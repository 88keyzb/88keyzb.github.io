Moving Out

I stayed at home a little longer than I would have initially thought.
Between covid, comfort, and my mother's food, it was hard to leave. Now that I finally did,
I see what I've been hearing about. 

Mimetic desire on the street. Credit cards. Politics as morality. Alcohol, drugs. Finding community.
Dating. Different definitions of the good life. TV shows, movies. Cooking. Rent. Transportation. Moving.

I do admit that I was naive in thinking that I would have a lot more time on my hands then before. I still attribute some of that to having
to move around a lot. But it still is mostly a matter of my habits. 

Practicing, do hard mental work in general, has been more difficult for me. Reading, math, practicing guitar and piano, writing. Having a balance 
of these things is something that I still am struggling to get a grasp on.

My place in the community, between new friends.

Something new also is having to solve problems when no one else is there. I do ask for help when needed, but sometimes it is just me.

Today I got a massive migraine, probably from food, bad sleep, weather, too much caffeine. I didn't have anyone to help, just me on my bad. It
was rough.
